Odoo's Default Product Page

No information about related products & recently Viewed Product

When visitors view any specific product, they don't see any suggestions for related products or a list of recently viewed items. This can lead to missed opportunities for cross-selling and upselling, as well as a less engaging shopping experience.

What we added

We added some very neccessery sections

we added two sections to the product page: "Related Products" and "Recently Viewed Products". The "Related Products" section displays products that are similar or complementary to the one being viewed, helping customers discover additional items they may be interested in. The "Recently Viewed Products" section shows a list of products that the customer has recently looked at, making it easy for them to navigate back to items they were considering. These additions enhance the shopping experience by providing personalized recommendations and improving product visibility, ultimately driving more sales.

How Related Product Conncected

Question is how the product are related

These related products are actually accesories product of the main product. When you add or edit an product there is a tab called Sales. In this tab you will find a field called "Accessory Products". You can add multiple products in this field. The products added in this field will be shown as related products in the product page of the main product.
